How protection works

Smart mode looks for your trigger words in page text. It does not recognize what an image or video shows.

Protection details and limits

Word matching

Matching ignores letter case and includes common English word forms:

  • Plurals — "spider" matches "spiders"
  • Verb forms — "fall" matches "fell"
  • Irregular plurals — "mouse" matches "mice"
  • Possessives — "spider" matches "spider's"

While pages load

Media may appear blurred while page text is checked. If the check fails, supported media stays blurred. Some content may still appear briefly before protection applies.

What gets blurred

  • Images, including GIFs
  • Background images
  • Videos
  • Embedded content, such as YouTube players

Some content inside custom page components and decorative backgrounds cannot be checked.

Content added later

New content, such as posts loaded as you scroll, is checked too. Media may stay blurred during the check.

Records protection decisions for troubleshooting. Logs omit page text, trigger words, and URLs.

To read logs, open developer tools (F12, or Cmd+Option+I on a Mac), select Console, and enable Verbose messages. Look for PhobiaBlur. Background and analysis logs appear in the extension’s service worker and offscreen document consoles.
